Physics Models & Theoretical Foundations

2.1 Off-Axis Top-Up Injection Principle

Traditional top-up injection employs pulsed dipole bump kickers, which introduce transient closed-orbit distortion and user experiment disruption due to pulse mismatch. The Nonlinear Kicker Magnet (NKM) generates a non-axisymmetric octupole-like magnetic field profile along the horizontal plane ($y=0$), producing zero field and zero spatial derivative at the closed orbit ($x=0$), while delivering a maximum kick deflection at the injected bunch position ($x \approx -16\text{ mm}$).

2.2 Analytical 4-Wire NLK Field Equation

The complex magnetic flux density $\vec{B}(x,y)$ produced by four symmetric conductors at $(\pm x_c, \pm y_c)$ carrying alternating currents $I$ is evaluated via Biot-Savart integration:

$$ B_y(x,y) = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi} \sum_{k=1}^{4} (-1)^{k} \frac{x - x_k}{(x - x_k)^2 + (y - y_k)^2} $$

The net horizontal deflection angle $\Delta x'$ experienced by ultra-relativistic 4.0 GeV electrons across length $L_{\text{nkm}} = 0.525\text{ m}$ is:

$$ \Delta x' = \frac{q}{p_0} \int_{0}^{L_{\text{nkm}}} B_y(x,y,z) \, dz = \frac{1}{(B\rho)_0} \int_{0}^{L_{\text{nkm}}} B_y(x,y,z) \, dz $$

2.3 2D Spline Field Map & Thick Symplectic Tracking

To model realistic RADIA 3D fringe fields and 2D kick maps without artificial numerical boundary noise, bicubic spline interpolation is used. Symplectic flow is maintained over $N_{\text{slices}}$ thick integration steps:

$$ x_{k+1/2} = x_k + \frac{1}{2} \Delta z \, x'_k, \quad x'_{k+1} = x'_k + \frac{q}{p_0} B_y\left(x_{k+1/2}, y_{k+1/2}\right) \Delta z, \quad x_{k+1} = x_{k+1/2} + \frac{1}{2} \Delta z \, x'_{k+1} $$

Authoritative Simulation Procedures (Steps 1–6)

Step 1: Field Validation

5-way cross-validation between RADIA 3D, RADIA 2D, analytical 4-wire, and polynomial fits.

Step 2: Tracking Convergence

Thick slicing study demonstrating $< 10^{-6}\text{ rad}$ convergence for $N_{\text{slices}} \ge 20$.

Step 3: Multi-Turn Injection

Evaluates 4 kicker models across 1,000 turns with physical apertures ($\pm 30\text{ mm} \times \pm 15\text{ mm}$).

Step 4: BTS Quad Matching

2-stage SLSQP matching of 8 quad families with SVD Jacobian decomposition.

Step 5: Tolerance Budget

500 Monte Carlo seeds with quad errors ($\sigma_{x,y} = 100\,\mu\text{m}$, $\sigma_\phi = 0.5\text{ mrad}$).

Step 6: MOGA Pareto Study

NSGA-II multi-objective optimization balancing mismatch, beta peak, and stay-clears.
