Nonlinear Kicker Magnet (NKM) & BTS Simulation Framework

Metadata: Author: Chong Shik Park | Department of Accelerator Science and Center for Accelerator Research, Korea University, Sejong, 30019 Republic of Korea.

This repository provides static documentation for the numerical modeling, 3D/2D RADIA magnetic field ingestion, thick symplectic particle tracking, multi-turn physical aperture storage ring injection dynamics, and multi-objective genetic algorithm (MOGA) optics optimization of the Nonlinear Kicker Magnet (NKM) system at the Pohang 4th Generation Storage Ring (POHANG 4GSR).

Physics Models & Theoretical Foundations

Off-Axis Top-Up Injection Principle

Traditional top-up injection employs pulsed dipole bump kickers, which introduce transient closed-orbit distortion and user experiment disruption due to pulse mismatch. The Nonlinear Kicker Magnet (NKM) generates a non-axisymmetric octupole-like magnetic field profile along the horizontal plane ($y=0$), producing zero field and zero spatial derivative at the closed orbit ($x=0$), while delivering a maximum kick deflection at the injected bunch position ($x \approx -16\text{ mm}$).

Analytical 4-Wire NLK Field Model

The complex magnetic flux density $\vec{B}(x,y)$ produced by four symmetric conductors at $(\pm x_c, \pm y_c)$ carrying alternating currents $I$ is evaluated via Biot-Savart integration: 

B_y(x,y) = (mu_0 * I / 2pi) * sum_{k=1}^4 (-1)^k * (x - x_k) / ((x - x_k)^2 + (y - y_k)^2)

The net horizontal deflection angle $\Delta x'$ experienced by ultra-relativistic 4.0 GeV electrons across length $L_{\text{nkm}} = 0.525\text{ m}$ is: 

Delta x' = (q / p_0) * int_{0}^{L_nkm} B_y(x,y,z) dz = (1 / (B\rho)_0) * int_{0}^{L_nkm} B_y(x,y,z) dz

2D Spline Field Map & Thick Symplectic Tracking

To model realistic RADIA 3D fringe fields and 2D kick maps without artificial numerical boundary noise, bicubic spline interpolation is used. Symplectic flow is maintained over $N_{\text{slices}}$ thick integration steps: 

x_{k+1/2} = x_k + 0.5 * dz * x'_k
x'_{k+1}   = x'_k + (q / p_0) * B_y(x_{k+1/2}, y_{k+1/2}) * dz
x_{k+1}   = x_{k+1/2} + 0.5 * dz * x'_{k+1}

Authoritative Simulation Procedures

  • Step 1: Field Validation — 5-way cross-validation between RADIA 3D, RADIA 2D, analytical 4-wire, and polynomial fits.
  • Step 2: Tracking Convergence — Thick slicing study demonstrating $< 10^{-6}\text{ rad}$ convergence for $N_{\text{slices}} \ge 20$.
  • Step 3: Multi-Turn Injection — Evaluates 4 kicker models across 1,000 turns with physical apertures ($\pm 30\text{ mm} \times \pm 15\text{ mm}$).
  • Step 4: BTS Quad Matching — 2-stage SLSQP matching of 8 quad families with SVD Jacobian decomposition.
  • Step 5: Tolerance Budget — 500 Monte Carlo seeds with quad errors ($\sigma_{x,y} = 100\,\mu\text{m}$, $\sigma_\phi = 0.5\text{ mrad}$).
  • Step 6: MOGA Pareto Study — NSGA-II multi-objective optimization balancing mismatch, beta peak, and stay-clears.
